Research Interests

Generative models for 3D molecules
Bayesian Flow Networks and diffusion models that generate atom coordinates and atom types together, conditioned on a protein pocket and respecting SE(3) symmetry.
Fast, training-free sampling
Cutting the number of network evaluations at inference time without retraining, through confidence-based sampling and ODE solvers for diffusion and Bayesian flows.
Aligning generators with drug-design goals
Preference optimization and reinforcement learning (DPO-style objectives, GRPO) for balancing binding affinity, drug-likeness, synthesizability and physical validity at the same time.
Theory behind the methods
Measure-theoretic analysis of what Bayesian flow and diffusion samplers, and the objectives used to fine-tune them, actually optimize.
